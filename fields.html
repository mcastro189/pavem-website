Explore Different Ways to Play

Not every soccer game needs a full-sized field. Smaller fields are a great option for players who enjoy faster games and playing with fewer people. Here are a few different ways to play.

5v5 Soccer

Perfect for smaller groups who enjoy quick games, more touches on the ball, and fast-paced action.

7v7 Soccer

A great option for players who want more space than 5v5 while still enjoying a smaller field.

Futsal

Futsal is played on a smaller court and focuses on close ball control, passing, and quick movement.

LA Galaxy Park - Carson

Location: Carson, California

Field Type: 5v5 Turf and Futsal Courts

LA Galaxy Park offers smaller soccer fields for players who enjoy fast-paced games. The location features five 5v5 turf fields and two futsal courts, making it a great option for playing with friends or joining smaller games. Court reservations are required.
