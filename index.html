How It Works

Finding a game should be simple. Sometimes you want to play soccer but don't have a full team or a group of friends available at the same time. PAVEM helps you explore pickup games and find opportunities to get back on the field.

01. Find a Game

Browse available pickup games around Orange County and find one that works with your schedule.

02. Check the Details

Review the location, game format, and other important details before joining.

03. Join and Play

Choose a game, reserve your spot, and get ready to enjoy soccer with other players.

More Than Just Games

PAVEM brings soccer players together to play, connect, and enjoy the game. Whether you're new to soccer or have played for years, everyone should have a place on the field.
