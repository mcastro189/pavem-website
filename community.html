The Soccer Community

Soccer is more than just a game. It's about meeting new people, making memories, and enjoying time on the field. PAVEM brings players around Orange County together through their love for soccer.

Whether you're just starting out or have been playing for years, there's always a place for you in the soccer community. We want people to feel welcome, connect with others, and enjoy playing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PAVEM?

PAVEM is a website concept that helps people explore soccer fields and connect with the soccer community around Orange County.

Do I need to be on a team?

No! You can explore local fields and find opportunities to enjoy soccer without being part of a team.

Where can I find soccer fields?

Visit our Find Field page to explore soccer locations around Orange County.
