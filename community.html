The Soccer Community

Soccer has a way of bringing people together. It can start with a few friends passing the ball around, turn into a pickup match, and become a regular part of the week. You don't need to know everyone before you arrive. Sometimes the best part of playing is meeting new people who enjoy the same game.

Orange County has players with different backgrounds, schedules, and levels of experience. Some people play to stay active, some want to improve their skills, and others just want to enjoy a match after a busy day. PAVEM helps bring those players together through their love for soccer.

Local Soccer Communities

Orange County and nearby cities have different soccer communities where people come together to play. Whether you're looking for a casual pickup game or a new group of players, these communities offer opportunities to get involved.

Callejeros FC

Callejeros FC is a local soccer group that hosts pickup games at DeForest Park in Long Beach. According to a local player, games take place on Mondays and Thursdays. It's another way for people to meet new players and enjoy soccer outside of organized leagues.

Get Involved

You can be competitive and still make room for fun. You can be new to a group and still feel welcome. The important thing is showing up, respecting other players, and enjoying the experience.

Whether you come with friends or arrive on your own, every game is an opportunity to meet people and enjoy soccer. Your next match could be the start of a new routine or a new connection.
